Experiment F: the two smallest Koios designs over newdesigns_rule.txt's limit (written 2026-11-18
01:09 EST, before any run of this experiment)

Designs: lstm (95,679 LUTs) and dla_like.small (93,939 LUTs). Both failed newdesigns_rule.txt on
memory (7.23 and 7.34 GiB peak in the width search) but route (newdesigns/bigtrial.txt), and they are
the two smallest Koios designs left: the 10 have 7-30k LUTs each (koios_sizes.txt). They are a separate test of
whether the split and the layer pass hold on much bigger designs, not designs 11 and 12: they do not
join the 10, and the 10-design tables do not change.

Runs: experiment C's set, exactly as the 3 added designs ran it (run_newdesigns.sh, POINTS
"k100 k162pin"): 10 seeds; stacked, split pass-off and split pass-on; routed and ideal clocks;
k = 1.0 (SPICE delays) and k = 1.62 + pins (the harshest reading of the layout). Same VPR, same
delay sets, same 1.3x track relaxation, pass-on at its pass-off twin's track count.
Not run (about 1.5 days more, newdesigns/bigtrial.txt): k = 1.62, 2.24, 2.72 and 3.0, the 2D fabric,
the ablation and equal tracks.

Reporting: both designs, both points, every seed, in their own table ("2 bigger designs") next to
the 10-design tables, whatever they show. A run that does not route within 50 router iterations is
rerun at 200 (run_failed.sh), as for the 10; a run that still fails drops only that seed. If the
sweep is stopped before it ends, what finished is reported and what is missing is named; no seed or
design is chosen afterwards.

Not run: dnnweaver (114k LUTs, 1221 RAMs), clstm_like.small (175k) and gemm_layer (396k), which are
bigger still.

Machine: the Mac (48 GiB). A width search peaks at about 7.3 GiB, so at most 5-6 VPR runs at once.
