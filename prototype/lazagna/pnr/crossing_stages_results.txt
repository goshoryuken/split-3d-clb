# Why the laid-out in-CLB crossing (504-760 ps) is 1.5-2.2x SPICE's (339 ps), stage by stage.
# Below: pnr/crossing_stages_layout.py's output, then the layout runs' log lines; every number here is from them.
#
# SPICE: spice/crossing_stages.py (run_crossing_stages.sh; output in spice/crossing_stages_3d20own_w100_tt
#   .json and .txt) builds make_spice.py's feedback deck with a measure at every stage, then
#   changes one thing at a time toward the layout's worst crossing path (pnr_local20c, FLE 0 to
#   layer 1): its bond-net, receiver-net and FLE-net loads, its standard cells (from the kit's CDL),
#   and the two buffers Innovus inserted. 'base' reproduces delays_tt.json's 339.23 within 0.1 ps.
# Layout: pnr_local20c laid out again (run_crossing_fix.sh), one change each, same RTL, split,
#   520 um core, mid-core pads and 2 ns clock (checked: split, RTL and constraints identical but
#   for the change):
#   s  each bond output port's set_load is what it drives on the other layer (bond_net_loads.py:
#      16-103 fF over the 20 FLE outputs, mean 38) instead of 6 fF
#   t  s, and no buffers inserted on the crossing nets in Innovus (0 found afterwards)
#   u  s, and timing budgets on the bond ports instead of 0 ns input/output delay, so each layer
#      laid out alone sees the crossing half-paths as critical as they are joined
#   Each layout's paths were also split at the crossbar mux's first pin (retime_joined.sh, the
#   mux_in column; the other columns are unchanged).
#
# What it shows:
# - SPICE given the layout's cells, loads and inserted buffers gives 583 ps for the crossing, and
#   0.84 of Tempus's stage delays on the same path: the two agree on where the time goes.
# - The real bond loads (s) and blocking inserted buffers (t) leave the layout's crossing at
#   0.735-0.750 ns: synthesis buffers instead (t), and the same-layer paths slow as much.
# - The cause is the per-layer setup: with 0 ns delay on the bond ports, each layer sees only half
#   of a crossing path, with the whole period to spare, so nothing on it is made fast. Budgets (u)
#   cut the crossing to 0.549 ns (same layer unchanged at 0.932), most of it before the mux. They
#   cost the CLB input pin paths: 1.363 vs 1.284 ns.
# - The rest is the standard-cell flow, not the crossing circuit: SPICE runs 0.84 of Tempus on the
#   same cells, the slower crossing nets also slow the standard-cell crossbar mux (+0.116 inside the
#   mux in u; SPICE's mux model barely differs), and the spread over sending FLEs (0.344-0.732 in u)
#   is placement.
# Defended range for the in-CLB crossing: 339 ps (SPICE, the same model as every other VPR delay)
# to 549 ps (the timing-driven layout, mean over 1,200 paths); SPICE with the layout's loads gives
# 294-463 ps. VPR at 1.36x (463 ps) and 1.62x (549 ps): hardblocks/results/seeds10/crossings/.
